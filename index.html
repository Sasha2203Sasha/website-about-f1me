🛞 Single-Lap Telemetry: Russell vs Antonelli

Python • FastF1 • Matplotlib • Barcelona 2026 Q

Question: Who is more aggressive — Russell or Antonelli? And how does driving style explain tyre degradation?

Finding: Russell is more aggressive on throttle application (higher mean change), yet he degrades tyres less in the race — a paradox. Antonelli is smoother but slower on a single lap and wears tyres faster over a stint.

Insight: Aggression alone doesn't destroy tyres. It's about staying within the grip limit. Russell attacks but doesn't exceed it.
