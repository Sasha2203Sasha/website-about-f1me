🧠 Key Findings

🥇 Only Antonelli Had the Right Strategy

Mercedes got it perfect for their rookie: 2 pit stops, laps 24 and 51. The model confirms this was within 0.7 seconds of the theoretical optimum.

📉 Ferrari — Worst Strategy of the Day

Hamilton and Leclerc both made 3 pit stops when the model says 1 stop on lap 29 was optimal. Combined loss: 194 seconds.

🔧 1 Stop Was Best for 7 of 8 Drivers

MEDIUM → HARD with a single stop around lap 29–30. The model with realistic tyre limits (30 laps for MEDIUM, 40 for HARD) confirms this is feasible and fastest.

🤖 How the Model Works

Linear regression on lap times vs tyre age for each driver. Brute-force search over all possible 1, 2, and 3-stop strategies. Realistic tyre age limits applied (SOFT: 20, MEDIUM: 30, HARD: 40 laps).
